Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial?
Yes — 14-day free trial on the Grow plan. No credit card required. Full access to all Grow features.
How does NGO discounting work?
Upload your 12A/80G registration certificate at checkout. We verify within 24 hours and apply 40% off permanently to your plan.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings. Upgrades are prorated immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
What's the difference between SaaS and a one-time license?
SaaS = we host it, you log in via browser. One-time license = you download the source code and install on your own server. You own it forever.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes — 7-day money-back on one-time licenses (if not deployed). Monthly SaaS: cancel anytime, access continues till end of period. Annual: 30-day full refund.
