FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SoulServe free to use?
Yes, we offer a free community edition for personal/educational use. For commercial NGO use, paid plans start at ₹999/month. Registered NGOs get 40% off — verified by 12A/80G certificate.
Can I self-host SoulServe on my own server?
Absolutely. One-time licenses (₹4,999–₹24,999) let you deploy on any LAMP/cPanel server. Your data stays on your server — no vendor lock-in.
How long does setup take?
Most NGOs are live within 60 minutes. Import the database, edit 2 config files, create admin account. Our detailed guide walks through every step.
What technology does it use?
PHP 8+, MySQL 8+, Bootstrap 5, JavaScript. Runs on any shared hosting or VPS. No special requirements — if you can run WordPress, you can run SoulServe.
Do you support multiple donation types?
Yes — food donations (with freshness/safe-hours tracking) and clothing donations (with condition/type classification). Both use the same 7-stage tracked pipeline.
Is there a white-label option for agencies?
Yes. The Agency License (₹24,999 one-time) gives unlimited deployments with full white-label rights — replace SoulServe branding with your client's name and logo.
