Rules

Donation Guidelines

✅ We Accept

  • ✔ Freshly cooked food (same day)
  • ✔ Sealed packaged food within expiry
  • ✔ Clean, wearable clothing
  • ✔ New or gently used clothes
  • ✔ Winter wear (jackets, sweaters, shawls)
  • ✔ Children's clothing in good condition

❌ We Do Not Accept

  • ✗ Stale, expired, or unsafe food
  • ✗ Partially eaten meals
  • ✗ Torn, unwashed, or unusable clothes
  • ✗ Items in very poor / damaged condition
  • ✗ Undergarments or personal hygiene items
  • ✗ Items without a photo upload
