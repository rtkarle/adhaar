Donation Guidelines

✓ What we accept

  • Freshly cooked food (within 4 hours)
  • Clean, wearable clothing in good condition
  • Kids clothes, winter wear, daily wear
  • Food prepared in hygienic conditions
  • Bulk donations welcome

✗ What we cannot accept

  • Stale, spoiled or contaminated food
  • Torn, dirty or unwearable clothing
  • Items that cannot be distributed safely
  • Non-food perishables without packaging
