Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is food pickup?⌄
We aim to pick up food donations within 2–4 hours of submission. For urgent cases, mark the priority as “High” in the form.
Do I need an account to donate?⌄
You need a free account to submit donations — this allows us to send you status updates and delivery proof by email.
How do I become a volunteer?⌄
Fill out the volunteer registration form on the homepage or contact page. Our team will reach out with your first pickup assignment.
Can I sell on the SoulServe Marketplace?⌄
Yes! Register as a Seller, set up your store, and list your products. Our team verifies stores before they go live.
How do I track my donation?⌄
After logging in, visit your Donor Dashboard. Every status update — accepted, scheduled, picked up, delivered — is tracked there and emailed to you.
Do you work with NGOs and CSR programs?⌄
Absolutely. We partner with NGOs, corporates and CSR programs for bulk donation drives. Email us at adhaarsoulserve@gmail.com.
