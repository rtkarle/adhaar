Frequently Asked Questions

How do I donate food or clothes?+
Register for a free account as a Donor, then go to your dashboard and click "Donate". Fill the form with your details and upload a photo. Our team reviews and schedules a pickup, usually within 24 hours.
How can I become a volunteer?+
Register on the Sign Up page and select "Volunteer" as your role. After email verification, you'll have access to the volunteer dashboard where you can accept pickup tasks assigned by the admin.
Can I sell my products on Adhaar?+
Yes! Register as a "Seller" and set up your store in the Seller Dashboard. You can list products with photos, prices, and descriptions. Buyers (donors and volunteers) can purchase directly from your store.
Is Adhaar free to use?+
Completely free for donors, volunteers, and buyers. Sellers list and sell with no platform fee during our launch phase. Our goal is impact, not profit extraction from those who need it most.
How do I track my donation after submitting?+
After submitting, log in to your Donor Dashboard and click "Track Donations". Every status change — Accepted, Scheduled, Out for Pickup, Delivered — triggers an email notification and updates your live tracker.
